# Finance YouTube: What dominates? Fear or frugality? 

The Research Question: Do panic-driven titles ("Recession Coming", "Market Crash", "Do THIS Now") drive higher view velocity than evergreen literacy titles ("How to Budget", "Index Funds Explained") during different market cycles?

Potential Candidates: 

1. Mark Tilbury (~9.0M)
Highest subscriber count and very high views-per-video. Money, business, and practical wealth content. Still posting in 2026 (lower frequency than daily channels, but consistent across the whole 2023–2026 window). Strong engagement. 

2. Graham Stephan (~5.2M)
Long-standing face of personal-finance + real-estate YouTube. Regular uploads throughout the period. High name recognition; engagement is solid rather than elite. Heavy sponsorships.

3. Caleb Hammer
Extremely active (thousands of videos, frequent long-form audits + clips). Highest raw view volume among personality channels. Confrontational “financial audit” format drives comments and watch time. Reputation is entertainment-first, not academic. 

4. Andrei Jikh (~3.3M)
Polished investing / dividend / portfolio content. Steady publisher since well before 2023. Frequently appears near the top of both popularity and brand-value lists.

5. Minority Mindset (Jaspreet Singh) (~2.5M)
Wealth-building and mindset + basics. Consistent output and strong community engagement.

6. Erika Kullberg (~2.3M)
Lawyer-turned-finance creator. High engagement rate on the July 2026 blended ranking. Active across the full window.

7. Nischa (~2.2M)
Personal-finance explainers with strong recent growth and solid average views.

8. Humphrey Yang (~2.1M)
Clear, structured personal-finance explainers (tax, accounts, inflation). Reliable cadence from 2023 onward. Often cited as a model for the niche.

9. Meet Kevin (Kevin Paffrath) (~2.0M)
One of the most active channels (daily or near-daily market/real-estate commentary plus lives). Volume of output is unmatched; reputation is more mixed (high energy, frequent takes).

10. The Plain Bagel (Richard Coffin) (~1.2M)
CFA + CFP. Among the most respected for calm, accurate investing and personal-finance explainers. Regular (not daily) publishing through 2026. High engagement for the size. 

11. Nate O’Brien (~1.3M)
Minimalism + personal finance. Steady, clean production and consistent uploads across the period.

12. Ben Felix (~600–650k) or The Money Guy Show (~700k)
Smaller subscriber counts but among the highest reputation scores. Ben Felix (PWL Capital) is the go-to evidence-based / academic-investing channel. The Money Guy Show (CFP team) is extremely active with planning-focused content and a large video library. Both published continuously 2023–2026.
